Management Information Systems (MIS) are becoming more and more relevant in today's business industry. MIS combines a variety of aspects that help any business operate effectively. These include people, information, data, etc. These systems allow managers to steer away from gut decisions and ones based on data. By organizing this information, businesses can improve communication and effectiveness of their daily operations. Artificial Intelligence is becoming more common in MIS because it gives people the opportunity to speed up processes and identify issues. Understanding these tools will give people the opportunity to witness how technology affects businesses in today's society.
